Features

Built specifically for Bangladesh's polytechnic and engineering students — with features that make textbook trading simple.

Smart Search & Filter
Find books by campus, subject, category, condition, and price range. Zero irrelevant results.
Real-time Chat
Message sellers directly with text and photos. No phone number sharing required.
Instant Notifications
Get alerts for new messages and when a book you requested becomes available.

How it works

About Boi Campus

A student-first marketplace built to make textbooks affordable and accessible for every student across Bangladesh.

About the App

Boi Campus is a dedicated book marketplace for Bangladesh's polytechnic, HSC, SSC, and university students. Buy, sell, or give away textbooks within your campus community — quickly and at prices that make sense for students.

Built by BanglaByteStudio, the app is designed to be lightweight, secure, and campus-aware — so every student in Bangladesh can find the books they need without leaving their campus area.
