About the App

Boi Campus is a student-first book marketplace built exclusively for Bangladesh's polytechnic and engineering students. The app lets you buy, sell, and exchange textbooks within your campus community — quickly, easily, and at affordable prices.


Whether you're looking for a specific semester textbook or want to sell books you no longer need, Boi Campus connects you with fellow students near your campus. No middlemen, no hassle — just students helping students.

Features

Smart Search & Filter

Find books by campus, department, semester, subject, condition, and price range. Powerful filters help you find exactly what you need.

Real-time Chat

Message sellers directly inside the app — no phone number sharing required. Send photos, negotiate prices, and arrange meetups safely.

Instant Push Notifications

Get notified immediately when you receive a new message or when a requested book becomes available on campus. Granular notification settings let you control what alerts you want.

Book Requests

Can't find a specific book? Post a request and get notified automatically when someone lists it. Never miss a book you're looking for.

Wishlist

Save books you're interested in to your wishlist and come back to them anytime. Never lose track of a great deal.

Post & Sell Books

List your textbooks in minutes — add photos, set a price, describe the condition, and choose your preferred delivery method. Your listing reaches all students on your campus.

Credit System

Post listings using ad credits. Earn free credits by watching short reward ads, or use existing credits to post. A fair system that keeps the app free for everyone.

Share Book Posts

Share a book listing with anyone — even people who haven't installed the app yet. They'll see a full preview with photos, price, and details.

Verified Profiles & Reports

OTP phone verification ensures all users are real. If you encounter any issue, you can report listings or users to keep the community safe.

How It Works

1

Create your account

Sign up with your phone number. Verify via OTP and complete your profile with your institute, campus, and department.

2

Browse or post

Search for books available near your campus with detailed filters — or list your own books with photos and a price in minutes.

3

Chat & close the deal

Message the seller directly through the in-app chat. Negotiate, agree on a price, and meet up on campus to exchange the book.

Advertising & Monetization

Boi Campus is completely free to download and use. Ads are how we keep the platform running. All ads are served by Google AdMob and follow Google's content policies. Ads never block critical user actions and we follow fair frequency limits so browsing stays enjoyable.

App Open Ad

Shown when you open the app after it has been in the background for at least 30 seconds. Designed to appear at natural app entry points, not during active use.

Max 2 per day Min. 4 hours between shows App must be backgrounded 30s+

Banner Ad

A small banner appears at the bottom of the Book Details screen and the Search screen. It never covers content and doesn't interfere with reading or navigation.

Book Details screen Search screen

Interstitial Ad (Full Screen)

A full-screen ad that may appear at natural navigation points — after successfully posting a book listing, or occasionally while browsing (every 5th book you open). Frequency is limited so browsing stays smooth.

After submitting a listing Every 5th book navigation Min. 3 minutes between shows

Rewarded Ad (Opt-in)

Completely optional. You can choose to watch a short ad to earn 1 free ad credit for posting a book listing. You are never forced to watch this ad — it's always your choice.

Always opt-in, never forced Earns 1 posting credit Max 5 per 24 hours

Ad Provider: Google AdMob

All ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may use device advertising ID for ad personalization. You can opt out via your device's Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Data & Privacy

Your Contact Info is Private

Your phone number is used only for OTP verification and is never shown to other users. Chat happens inside the app — no number sharing needed.

Secure Data Transmission

All data between the app and our servers is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS). Authentication is handled by Supabase with row-level security policies.

No Unnecessary Data Collection

We collect only what's needed to make the app work: your phone number (auth), profile info you provide, and book listings you create. We do not sell your data.
