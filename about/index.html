§ 01

About the App

§ 01

Boi Campus (বই ক্যাম্পাস) is a student-first book marketplace built exclusively for Bangladesh's polytechnic and engineering students. The app lets you buy, sell, and exchange textbooks within your campus community — quickly, easily, and at prices that actually make sense for students.

Whether you're looking for a specific semester textbook or want to sell books you no longer need, Boi Campus connects you with fellow students near your campus. No middlemen, no hassle — just students helping students.

Completely free to download and use. The app is supported by ads, which keeps the platform running at no cost to students.

§ 02

Features

§ 02

Everything you need to buy and sell textbooks on campus — built specifically for Bangladeshi students.

Smart Search & Filter Filter by campus, department, semester, condition & price to find exactly what you need.
Real-time Chat Message sellers directly inside the app. Send photos, negotiate prices, and arrange meetups safely.
Push Notifications Instant alerts for new messages and when a requested book becomes available on campus.
Book Requests Can't find a book? Post a request and get notified automatically when someone lists it.
Wishlist Save books you're interested in and revisit them anytime — never miss a good deal.
Post & Sell Books List your books in minutes with photos, price, condition and preferred delivery method.
Credit System Post listings using ad credits. Earn free credits by watching short reward ads.
Share Book Posts Share any listing with anyone — even people who haven't installed the app get a full preview.
Verified Profiles Email OTP verification ensures all users are real. Report listings or users to keep the community safe.

§ 03

How It Works

§ 03

Getting started with Boi Campus takes less than 2 minutes. Here's how it works:

1 · Create Your Account
Sign up with your email. Verify via OTP, then complete your profile with your institute, campus, and department.
2 · Browse or Post a Book
Search for books near your campus with detailed filters — or list your own books with photos and a price in minutes.
3 · Chat & Close the Deal
Message the seller directly through in-app chat. Negotiate a price, agree on a meetup spot, and exchange the book.

§ 05

App Permissions

§ 05

Boi Campus requests only the permissions that are strictly necessary for the app to function.

Permission Platform Why It's Needed
Camera Android Taking photos directly from chat to send to buyers or sellers. Never accessed in the background.
Photo Library Android Selecting existing photos from your gallery for book listings and chat messages.
Notifications Both Receiving push alerts for new messages and book request matches. Requested once — you can decline.
Internet Both Required for all core features: loading listings, chat, notifications, and syncing data.

You can revoke any permission from your device's App Settings at any time. Revoking Camera or Gallery will prevent photo uploads. Revoking notifications will stop push alerts.

§ 06

Advertising & Monetization

§ 06

Boi Campus is a free app funded in part by advertisements served through Google AdMob. Ads help us keep the platform running at no cost to students.

Types of Ads Displayed
App Open
On cold start or after 30s+ in background. Max 2/day, min 4h between.
Banner
Fixed at bottom of Book Details and Search screens. Never blocks content.
Interstitial
Full-screen between transitions. Every 5th navigation, min 3 min apart.
Rewarded
Opt-in only. Watch to earn 1 posting credit. Max 5 per 24 hours.
Ad Provider
Google AdMob
All ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may use your device advertising ID for personalization. Opt out via Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization. Full policy: policies.google.com/privacy ↗

Ads never block critical user actions. We follow strict frequency limits so your browsing experience stays enjoyable.
