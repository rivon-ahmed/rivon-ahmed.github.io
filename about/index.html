Section 01

About the App

01

Boi Campus (বই ক্যাম্পাস) is a student-first book marketplace built exclusively for Bangladesh's polytechnic and engineering students. Buy, sell, and exchange textbooks within your campus community — quickly, easily, and at prices that actually make sense for students.

Whether you're looking for a specific semester textbook or want to sell books you no longer need, Boi Campus connects you with fellow students near your campus. No middlemen, no hassle — just students helping students.

Boi Campus is completely free to download and use. The app is supported by ads, which keeps the platform running at no cost to students.

Section 02

Features

02

Everything you need to buy and sell textbooks on campus — built specifically for Bangladeshi students.

Smart Search & Filter
Filter by campus, department, semester, condition & price to find exactly what you need.
Real-time Chat
Message sellers directly inside the app. Send photos, negotiate, and arrange meetups safely.
Push Notifications
Instant alerts for new messages and when a requested book becomes available on campus.
Book Requests
Can't find a book? Post a request and get notified automatically when someone lists it.
Wishlist
Save books you're interested in and revisit them anytime — never miss a good deal.
Post & Sell Books
List your books in minutes — add photos, set a price, describe condition, choose delivery method.
Credit System
Post listings using ad credits. Earn free credits by watching short reward ads.
Share Book Posts
Share a listing with anyone — even people who haven't installed the app get a full preview.
Verified Profiles
Email OTP verification ensures all users are real. Report listings or users to keep the community safe.

Section 03

How It Works

03
1
Create Your Account
Sign up with your email address. Verify via OTP and complete your profile — add your institute, campus, and department.
2
Browse or Post a Book
Search for books near your campus with powerful filters — or list your own books with photos and a price in just a few minutes.
3
Chat & Close the Deal
Message the seller directly through in-app chat. Negotiate a price, agree on a meetup spot, and exchange the book on campus.

Section 06

Advertising & Monetization

06

Boi Campus is completely free. Ads are how we keep the platform running. All ads are served by Google AdMob and follow Google's content policies. Ads never block critical user actions.

Ad Types
App Open Ad
Shown when you open the app after it has been in the background for 30+ seconds. Appears at natural entry points, not during active use.
Max 2 per day Min. 4 hours between shows App must be backgrounded 30s+
Banner Ad
A small banner at the bottom of the Book Details screen and the Search screen. Never covers content and doesn't interfere with navigation.
Book Details screen Search screen
Interstitial Ad (Full Screen)
A full-screen ad at natural navigation points — after successfully posting a listing, or every 5th book opened. Frequency is strictly limited.
After submitting a listing Every 5th book navigation Min. 3 minutes between shows
Rewarded Ad Opt-in
Completely optional. Watch a short ad to earn 1 free posting credit. You are never forced to watch this ad — it's always your choice.
Always opt-in, never forced Earns 1 posting credit Max 5 per 24 hours
Ad Provider: Google AdMob
All ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may use your device advertising ID for personalization. Opt out via Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Section 07

Data & Privacy

07
Your Contact Info is Private
Your phone number is used only for OTP verification and is never shown to other users. All communication happens inside the app.
Secure Data Transmission
All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS). Authentication is handled by Supabase with row-level security policies enforced on every query.
Minimal Data Collection
We collect only what's needed: your email (auth), profile info you provide, and book listings you create. We do not sell your data.
